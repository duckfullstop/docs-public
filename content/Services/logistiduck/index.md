# What I promise
Door-to-door movement of anything that isn't a person - for that you'll need a taxi.
# What I do
* I'll move most things from A to B, anywhere in Europe, in a battery electric vehicle.
* So long as they fit in a regular car (approx. 140x100x50cm max, but do enquire), I can move it.
	* Things larger than a car that require a van are possible, but will incur increased charges - please contact.
* I can handle routes requiring extra protocols, such as across international borders and sealed transit
	* I've done runs into the House of Commons requiring a sealed vehicle!
* I can also arrange postage, or anything else that would vaguely fall into the realm of a production runner.
# What I don't do
* Provide formal guarantees or warranty for the item(s) being moved. I'll do my utmost best to look after it, and will endeavour to make things right should the worst happen, but I can't formally cover losses that may arise.
* Move anything illegal / illicit (immoral, on the other hand...)
* Move things larger than a car across international borders, except in exceptional cases - this is due to van hire restrictions in the United Kingdom.
# My background
I've had a full license for 10+ years, and proudly hold an IAM Roadsmart Advanced Driver qualification at the highest standard grade.

I've been making logistics happen for various folks (queer and not!) for the last few years as a bit of a side gig. Strangely, moving stuff for people gives me a certain amount of self-satisfaction.

## A note on ability
Please note that I do have issues walking or standing for extended periods, and may occasionally make use of a stool or stick similar to reduce pain.

_Thank you for your understanding._ 🙇

# Pricing
logistiduck. has two rates - a distance rate, and an hourly rate.

The distance rate applies while travelling. The hourly rate applies for any tasks required at the origin and destination beyond simply loading / unloading the item(s).

**These rates are indicative, and not final. Please ask for a quote.**

## Distance Rate
Distance starts at **£0.75/mi**.

This rate can vary, and is influenced by various factors - very short runs may be more expensive - extremely long runs may be cheaper, depending on the complexity of the journey (international borders / ferry costs etc).

## Hourly Rate
The standard hourly rate for _logistiduck._ is **£20p/h**. This is billed to the 15 minute increment.

Please note that if a task is more technical than simple runner activities, there may be a pricing uplift - this will be discussed at quotation if it's applicable to you.
