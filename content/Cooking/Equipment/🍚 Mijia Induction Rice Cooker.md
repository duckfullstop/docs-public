My beloved 💙

Here lives a brief guide on using the Mijia IH Electric Rice Cooker. This is the one with the horrifying WiFi interface - please don't pair it to anything unless you'd like the Chinese government to have direct access to your smart fridge.

## How make rice!?!?
1. Measure rice using cup provided - 1 cup is approximately one portion
2. Rinse rice thoroughly in a separate bowl, the least starch the better
3. Put rice in cooker bowl, top water up to the mark in the cooker corresponding to the number of cups of rice you used (if confused which lines to use, google translate can help)
4. Plug in (using C13 cable)
5. Select mode using left hand button, you probably want “rice” which is the default
6. Optionally set end time using labelled touch controls on left
7. Press big go button in middle
8. Wait around an hour, the time will adjust about half way through
9. Once it’s finished, a little jingle plays, and it goes into keep warm mode - press stop to turn off

## Cleaning
Open cooker and remove bowl, then use clip on the inside of the lid to remove the sealing plate - behind that is a small white cartridge which can also be removed, and unclipped to reveal two halves.

Wash all of the above BY HAND - for the love of all that is holy, don’t dishwash because it’ll ruin the coating on the bowl, and I will cry :(
